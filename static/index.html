System Architecture & Methodology

How CrowdGuard Predicts Stampedes & Severe Traffic Jams

Rather than waiting for an accident to happen, CrowdGuard fuses three independent signals with road/gate width geometry to forecast bottlenecks 10 to 20 minutes early and automatically notify both Police Command (via n8n email + dashboard) and approaching commuters.

Signal 1 — Mobility & Net Flow

How We Calculate GPS Headcount & ETA to Red

We ingest anonymized zone-level device counts (gps_headcount), entry rate (inflow_per_min), exit rate (outflow_per_min), and average movement speed (speed_kmh).

Net_Flow = Inflow_per_min - Outflow_per_min
Density = Blended_Count / (Road_Width_m * 40m)
ETA_to_Red = (Safe_Capacity - Blended_Count) / Net_Flow

When speed drops below 3 km/h while Net Flow remains positive, a stagnation penalty elevates the zone to Yellow or Red before a crush occurs.

Signal 2 — Crowdsourced Reports & Anti-Spam

How User Posts & Confidence % Are Evaluated

A single unverified citizen post never causes a false city-wide panic. When a user captures a photo and submits a report, the backend checks sensor agreement between GPS and CCTV counts and adds a corroboration bonus per independent report:

Sensor_Agreement = 1 - |GPS - CCTV| / Blended_Count
Confidence_% = (Sensor_Agreement * 72) + min(Reports * 7, 25)

Reports also only notify commuters whose active route matches that corridor, preventing alert fatigue.

Signal 3 — Optical Density & Choke-Point Width

How CCTV Cameras & Road/Gate Width Work Together

1,000 people on a 14-meter road is manageable, whereas 1,000 people pressing into a 6.5-meter stadium gate (like Chinnaswamy Gate 3) is critical. Every zone stores its physical width (road_width_m) and safe capacity.

During scheduled events (matches, concerts, peak hours), the AI pre-loads the venue schedule and identifies idle police units at low-load gates (such as Gate 1) so commanders can reallocate them in one click.

Privacy & Governance

Addressing Privacy Risks & Surveillance Concerns

  • No Personal GPS Tracking: The backend never stores individual phone numbers, user identities, or historical movement trajectories—only aggregate zone counts.
  • No Facial Recognition: Camera feeds and citizen photos are evaluated strictly for crowd/traffic density, never biometric identification.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Police Override: Every automated alert can be acknowledged, escalated, rerouted, or dismissed manually by Police Command.

Automated Police Dispatch Integration

n8n Police Email Webhook Configuration

Ready to Connect

Whenever a citizen posts a photo report or a zone escalates, FastAPI sends a structured JSON webhook to n8n, which immediately emails the Bengaluru Police Control Room with the location, AI crowd summary, confidence score, and recommended unit action.

How to Set Up Your n8n Workflow in 2 Minutes:
  1. In n8n, create a new workflow and add a Webhook trigger node: set HTTP Method to POST and Path to police-alert. Copy the Production/Test URL into the box above.
  2. Connect a Gmail or Send Email node after the Webhook node.
  3. Set To Email to {{ $json.body.police_email_to }}, Subject to [CrowdGuard {{ $json.body.priority }}] {{ $json.body.zone_name }}, and Message to {{ $json.body.ai_summary }} — Action: {{ $json.body.recommended_police_action }}.
