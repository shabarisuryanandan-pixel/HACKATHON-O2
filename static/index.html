Simple & Transparent System Overview

How 4sight Prevents Stampedes & Severe Traffic Jams

Stampedes and 45-minute gridlocks happen when thousands of people keep moving into a narrow road or gate without knowing it is already full. 4sight combines four simple inputs to spot the buildup early—alerting the public not to go that way and helping police act before an accident occurs.

1. GPS Headcount

Live Crowd & Commuter Volume

Counts how many active mobile devices are currently inside a junction or stadium entry and how fast they are moving. If the count rises rapidly while movement slows down, the system knows a bottleneck is starting.

2. CCTV Cameras

Visual Crowd Count vs. Road Width

Security and traffic cameras estimate how packed a street or gate is compared to its physical width. For example, 1,200 people in a narrow 6.5-meter stadium gate is flagged as high risk, while 500 people on an 11-meter road is marked partially safe.

3. Human Reports

Real Photos From People on the Ground

Anyone stuck in a crowd or traffic jam can click a photo in the app. Because the system checks human reports alongside GPS and CCTV counts (with 30 to 40+ people reporting in busy zones), fake reports cannot trigger false panic.

4. AI Prediction

Early Warnings Before a Crush Happens

By combining GPS Headcount, CCTV Cameras, and Human Reports, the AI predicts where a choke point or stampede risk will form 15 to 20 minutes early.

Instant Dual Action: Public + Police

For the Public: Anyone heading toward that area gets an immediate alert on their phone telling them to be careful or take a clear bypass road so they do not add to the crowd.

For the Police: The Police Dashboard updates immediately and an automated alert is sent to the Police Control Room so officers can open relief gates or move idle units from quiet entries.

Built for Complete Public Privacy

No Personal Tracking: 4sight only measures total headcount in a zone—it never tracks personal identities, phone numbers, or private travel history.

No Facial Recognition: Camera feeds and citizen photos are used strictly to estimate crowd size and traffic flow, keeping citizens completely anonymous.
